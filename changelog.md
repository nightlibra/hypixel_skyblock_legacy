# ---- v2.0.0 ----
# Project Scope
My original plan for SkyBlock Legacy was just to preserve SkyBlock's original art direction, nothing more. I never intended for it to include any new unofficial textures. However, with nearly 200k downloads, people reaching out, and the assistance of an amazing artist, new art for future SkyBlock items that never had a legacy texture is now within this project's scope!

This update wouldn't be possible without **chndrh**'s phenomenal art contributions, or without **Portoss** getting the ball rolling with their sprayonator textures. Thank you both! ❤️

For those interested, I've created a new resource pack - [SkyBlock Classic](https://modrinth.com/resourcepack/hypixel-skyblock-classic) - which will act as an archive of strictly Hypixel SkyBlock's original art direction and textures.
